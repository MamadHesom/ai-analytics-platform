# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Participants should communicate respectfully, assume good intent while addressing impact, provide actionable feedback, and welcome different technical perspectives. Keep discussions focused on the work, protect private information, and help others reproduce and understand problems.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, and unwelcome sexualized or hostile conduct are not acceptable. Technical disagreement is welcome; disrespect is not.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Reports may be made privately to the project owners. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect confidentiality where possible, and may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a safe community.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
